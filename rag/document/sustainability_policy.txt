Sustainability Decision Framework

Organizations should continuously identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary
paper consumption, avoid repeated document printing, and encourage digital-first
workflows where appropriate.

Duplex printing should be considered where operationally appropriate.

Color printing should be used when color provides meaningful business value.
Organizations may review high color-print usage to identify opportunities for
black-and-white alternatives.

Repeated printing and failed print jobs should be monitored because they can
indicate workflow inefficiencies or user errors.

Sustainability recommendations should be reviewed by an appropriate human
decision maker before operational implementation.

Sustainability initiatives should be monitored after implementation to determine
whether the intended improvement actually occurred.

Metrics can include page volume, reprint rate, failed-print rate, color-print
rate, paper consumption, energy consumption and estimated waste reduction.
